Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026

Preflop Gym ("the app", "we") is a study tool for No-Limit Texas Hold'em preflop decisions, published by Stanislav Kozlovskii. This policy explains what data the app handles and why. The short version: the app has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your training data stays on your device.

Data stored on your device

The app keeps the following only in its private storage on your phone:

  • your answers in drills and exams, used for accuracy, streaks, mastery and the leak finder;
  • ranges you paint in the Range Builder;
  • your settings: daily goal, rake profile, stakes, reminder time, language.

This data is never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are sold through Google Play or the Apple App Store. The store processes payment; we never see your card or billing details.

To check whether Pro is active on your device and to restore purchases, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service provider. RevenueCat receives:

  • a random app user ID generated on your device (not linked to your name, email or store account);
  • the purchase receipt and transaction details from the store;
  • basic device and app information such as operating system, app version, country and IP address.

This data is used only to deliver and restore your purchases. RevenueCat processes it on our behalf under its own privacy policy. Google's handling of Play purchases is described in the Google Privacy Policy; Apple's in the Apple Privacy Policy.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device. No push service and no server are involved.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect your name, email, contacts, location, photos or any other personal data.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and we do not use the advertising ID.
  • We do not sell or share data with third parties for advertising.

No gambling

The app does not offer real-money play, wagering, prizes or any connection to gambling services.

Children

The app is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly process data of children.

Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data, there is usually nothing for us to access or delete. Training data is deleted by uninstalling the app or using "Reset progress" in Profile. If you want the purchase records linked to your anonymous RevenueCat app user ID deleted, email us at stan.kozlovskii@gmail.com; note that the store keeps its own purchase records. If you are in the EU, UK or another region with data protection laws, you may also contact your local supervisory authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
