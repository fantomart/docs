Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Split into 2–8 teams. On your turn, explain the word on the card without saying it or its root; your team guesses. Swipe up when they get it, down to skip. When the timer runs out the phone goes to the next team. After the round you can remove any disputed word with one tap.

Does it need the internet or an account?

No account. The game works fully offline. The internet is used only to show store prices and to buy or restore packs.

Why don’t words repeat?

The app remembers which words were already shown on this device and skips them until the whole pack has been played. Every pack shows how many words are left.

I bought a pack but it is still locked

Open Settings → Restore purchases, signed in to the same Google Play or App Store account you bought with. If that does not help, email us with the store receipt.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store: Google Play, Apple. Write to us if something went wrong and we will help where we can.

What is in the 18+ pack?

Adult humour, sex-related words and jokes, text only (no images). It opens only after everyone playing confirms they are 18 or older, and its words appear only when you select the pack.

How do I make my own pack?

Packs → Create pack, paste a list of words (one per line), optionally mark difficulty. Custom packs are free and stored on your device.

  • Возврат денег оформляет стор: Google Play, Apple.
  • Свой набор: Наборы → создать, вставьте список слов по одному на строку.
