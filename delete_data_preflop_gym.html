Delete your data

How to delete data of the Preflop Gym: GTO Poker Trainer app. Preflop Gym has no accounts, so there is nothing to sign in to or close.

1. Training data on your phone

Answers, statistics, streak, painted ranges and settings are stored only on your device.

  • Open the app → Profile → Reset progress → confirm. This deletes answers, statistics, streak and exams immediately.
  • Or uninstall the app. Android deletes all of its data at once.

2. Purchase records at RevenueCat

If you bought Pro, RevenueCat stores a random app user ID with your purchase receipts so that Pro can be restored.

  1. Email stan.kozlovskii@gmail.com with the subject "Preflop Gym data deletion".
  2. Include the Google Play order number of your purchase (it starts with GPA., you find it in the Google Play receipt email or in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history).
  3. We delete the matching customer record in RevenueCat within 30 days and confirm by email.

Deleted: the app user ID, purchase receipts and transaction history held by RevenueCat for that customer. After deletion, "Restore purchases" will re-create the record from your Google Play account the next time you use it.

Kept: Google keeps its own order records under the Google Privacy Policy; we cannot delete them. Active subscriptions are not cancelled by a deletion request; cancel them in Google Play first.
