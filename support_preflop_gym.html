Support

Questions, bugs, a range that looks wrong? Write to stan.kozlovskii@gmail.com. We usually answer within a day.

Pro does not show after reinstalling or on a new phone

Open Profile and tap "Restore purchases". Make sure you are signed in to the same Google Play or Apple account you bought Pro with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Android: Google Play app → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Preflop Gym → Cancel.
iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Preflop Gym → Cancel Subscription.
Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store. Android: request it on play.google.com order history. iPhone: reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my data?

All training data lives on your phone. Use Profile → Reset progress, or uninstall the app. See the Privacy Policy for purchase records.

Which games are the ranges for?

6-max No-Limit Hold'em, 100 big blinds, 2.5x opens. The micro-stakes rake profile tightens defends for NL2–NL10. The ranges are hand-authored approximations, not live solver output.
