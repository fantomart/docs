Alias Party is an offline word party game for iOS and Android: one phone, teams take turns explaining words. This policy describes what data the app handles.

1. The short version

  • No account, no sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email, contacts or location.
  • Your settings, team names, scores, custom word packs and the list of words already played are stored only on your device.
  • No ads, no advertising identifiers, no analytics, no crash reporting.
  • Data leaves the device only when you buy or restore a word pack through Google Play or the App Store.

2. Data stored on your device

The app keeps its state in the app’s private storage: game settings, language, theme, sound and vibration preferences, team names, the current game and scoreboard, custom packs you create, the history of words already shown (so they do not repeat), which packs you own and which free trial rounds you have used.

We never receive this data. Uninstalling the app deletes it. On Android, the system may include app settings in the device backup to your Google account if you have backup turned on; on iOS, app data is part of the device backups you configure (iCloud or computer). Those backups are governed by Google’s and Apple’s terms.

3. Purchases

Paid word packs are one-time purchases processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details.

To check which packs you own, the app uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.), a purchase-management service. RevenueCat generates a random anonymous app identifier on your device (not tied to your name, email or store account) and receives from the store your purchase receipts: product, date and transaction identifiers. RevenueCat’s policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

4. Rating prompt

The app may show the system “Rate this app” dialog of Google Play or the App Store. That dialog is provided by the store; we do not receive your rating or any data from it.

7. Children and the 18+ pack

The app is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone. The optional 18+ word pack contains adult, sex-related words and jokes (text only) and opens only after an age confirmation.

8. Your rights

All game data is on your device and you control it: delete the app to erase it. Purchase records are kept by Google, Apple and RevenueCat under their policies. For any question about your data email stan.kozlovskii@gmail.com.

9. Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date.
