Pocketfold is a personal travel planner. 5. Document recognition — accuracy disclaimer

Recognition is performed by a machine-learning service (Google Gemini) and is probabilistic. It can misread digits, dates, times, airport codes, names and currencies, or fill in the wrong field. Recognised values are suggestions to be checked, not facts. Always compare what the App shows with the original ticket or booking before relying on it. We are not responsible for missed flights, wrong check-in dates or other consequences of unverified recognised data.

Only scan documents you are entitled to scan. Do not use the feature for bulk processing or for documents unrelated to your travel.
